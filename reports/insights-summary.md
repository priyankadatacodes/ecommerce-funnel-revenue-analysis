# Insights Summary  
E-Commerce Funnel & Revenue Leakage Analysis

## Overview
This document summarizes the key business insights derived from the Tableau dashboard built on the order-level analytics table.  
All insights are directly supported by the data and visuals in the dashboard.

---

## Funnel Performance
- The order funnel is highly efficient, with **97.81% of orders successfully delivered**.
- Out of **100,213 total orders**, **98,016 reach the delivered stage**, indicating minimal drop-off.
- Orders rarely remain stuck in intermediate stages such as processing or invoiced, showing smooth order flow.

---

## Cancellations
- The **cancellation rate is only 0.47%**, which is very low for an e-commerce business at this scale.
- Cancelled orders form a negligible portion of total order volume.
- Cancellations are not currently a major operational or revenue risk.

---

## Revenue Performance
- The business generated **13.59M in total revenue**.
- Revenue loss due to cancellations is **97,243**, which represents only **0.72% of total revenue**.
- Most revenue is realized from delivered orders, confirming strong financial performance.

---

## Delivery Performance
- A significant share of delivered orders falls into the delayed category.
- Despite delivery delays, high conversion and low cancellation rates indicate that delays are **not leading to order abandonment**.
- Delivery timelines remain the most important operational area for improvement.

---

## Product Category Contribution
- Revenue is concentrated in a small number of product categories.
- Categories such as **beauty & health, watches & gifts, and home essentials** contribute a large share of total revenue.
- Any delivery or seller issues in these categories would have a higher financial impact.

---

## Seller Performance
- Seller delivery performance varies significantly across the platform.
- A small group of sellers shows consistently higher average delivery times.
- These sellers represent operational risk and should be prioritized for performance improvement.

---

## Key Business Takeaways
- The e-commerce funnel is performing very well with minimal revenue leakage.
- Financial risk from cancellations is low.
- Delivery delays and seller-level performance are the main opportunities for operational optimization.
- Focusing on high-revenue categories and slower sellers can deliver the highest business impact.

---

## Conclusion
The analysis confirms strong funnel health and revenue stability.  
Future improvements should focus on **reducing delivery delays and improving seller performance**, rather than changing the order funnel itself.
